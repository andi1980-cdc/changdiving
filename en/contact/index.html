🧭 How to Use Our Website

Booking your dive with us is easy!

  1. Use the Search or Menu – If you already know what you're looking for, click the 🔍 search icon in the menu bar of the homepage and type the course or trip name. Or open the menu and choose between Diving Courses (OWD, AOWD, Rescue, etc.) and Diving Day Trips (fun dives, snorkeling, try dives, etc.).
  2. Switch Language – Click on the flags in the top menu to change the website language to English, Deutsch, or ไทย.
  3. Return Home – Click the Chang Diving Center logo at the top of any page to go back to the homepage.
  4. Find Your Course or Trip – Browse the list and click on the activity you’re interested in. Use the submenu (appears after clicking Courses or Day Trips) to navigate between different activities. On mobile, you can swipe the submenu left or right to see all options. Check the breadcrumbs at the top to see where you are on the site.
  5. Get the Details – On each course or trip page, you’ll find all the important information, prices, and schedules.
  6. Book by Email – Below the price section, click the “Book Now by Email” button. Fill out the form (opens in your email) with your details and send it to us. That’s it! We’ll reply shortly with your booking confirmation. 🐠
Expect our response within 24 hours – please check your spam folder if you don't hear from us.
