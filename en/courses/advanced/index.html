Advanced Open Water Diver Course Koh Chang

The Advanced Open Water Diver course in Koh Chang is your next step after Open Water certification, upgrading your maximum depth from 18 meters to 30 meters and expanding your diving skills through 5 adventure dives. In just 2 days, you'll complete mandatory Deep Dive (30m) and Underwater Navigation training, plus 3 elective adventure dives at exciting sites in the Mu Koh Chang National Marine Park.

This AOWD course in Koh Chang is taught by experienced SDI, TDI, and PADI certified instructors who guide you through practical skills including deep diving procedures, precise underwater navigation, peak performance buoyancy, and specialty dives like wreck diving on the HTMS Chang wreck or Nitrox diving. Each adventure dive focuses on a specific skill area while allowing you to explore Thailand's diverse underwater environments and marine life.

Our Advanced diving course in Koh Chang, Thailand follows a flexible, experience-based approach with small groups and personalized instruction. Unlike the Open Water course with confined water sessions, the Advanced course is entirely open water diving – no pool training required. You'll dive at varied sites including deep reefs (up to 30m), shallow training areas for navigation, and famous locations like the HTMS Chang wreck, gaining confidence and skills that open access to more challenging dive sites worldwide.

This Advanced Open Water Diver course is perfect if you:

  • recently completed your Open Water certification and want to continue
  • want to dive deeper (30m) and access sites like the HTMS Chang wreck
  • prefer learning by doing – all training is through fun adventure dives
  • are interested in specialties like wreck diving, Nitrox, or advanced buoyancy
  • want to build experience and confidence before fun diving independently

All Advanced courses are conducted from our dive boat with professional equipment, small group sizes, and a focus on practical skill development. After certification, you'll be qualified to dive to 30 meters worldwide, explore Koh Chang's 13+ dive sites including deeper wrecks and reefs, and continue your diving education with specialty courses or Rescue Diver training.

If you're ready to upgrade from Open Water to Advanced and unlock deeper, more exciting dive sites in Koh Chang, Thailand, our Advanced Open Water Diver course gives you the skills, experience, and confidence to take your diving to the next level.

FAQs – Advanced Open Water Diver Course

Do I have to study theory again?

Yes, a short theory module is part of the course – but there's no big final exam like in Open Water. You can study everything online in advance.

How deep can I dive after the course?

As an AOWD diver, you're certified to dive up to 30 meters (Junior AOWD up to 21 meters).

Are the Adventure Dives the same as Specialty Courses?

No – Adventure Dives are introductory dives. You can credit them toward full Specialty Courses like Wreck Diver or Nitrox later.

What equipment do I need?

Everything is included in the course price – all you need is swimwear and a good mood!

What comes after AOWD?

You can continue with the Rescue Diver course or enroll in full Specialties. We're happy to advise you!
