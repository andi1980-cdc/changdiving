Learn to Dive in Koh Chang – Beginner Courses from Try Dive to Open Water

Start your scuba journey in the warm, clear waters of Koh Chang. At Chang Diving Center our expert SDI, TDI and PADI instructors teach beginner-friendly courses in small groups with modern equipment—perfect for couples, families and solo travelers.

Just curious? Begin with a relaxed Try Dive (no experience needed). Want a first certification with a pro? See the Scuba Diver option on our Open Water Diver page (dives to 12 m with a pro). Ready to dive independently worldwide? Go for the Open Water Diver Course (3 days, skills + reef dives to 18 m).

During training you’ll visit some of Koh Chang’s best dive sites—colorful reefs and gentle pinnacles—with a focus on comfort, safety and fun. Not sure what you might see? Explore our Marine Life Guide.

Prefer to taste diving first? Join a Day Trip or Fun Dives (for certified divers). For planning, check the How-to Guides and our course FAQ (requirements, schedule, what to bring, Nitrox).
