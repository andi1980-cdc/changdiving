Who are beginner diving courses for?

Never dived? Start with a Try Dive or go straight into Open Water if you’re ready to study theory and skills. Already tried elsewhere? Continue with Open Water or a combo package. Families, couples and solo travelers all train in the same beginner-friendly format — see course FAQ for age, medical and scheduling questions.

Try Dive, Scuba Diver, or Open Water — what should you choose?

Try Dive — no license, two boat dives, full supervision. Scuba Diver (on the Open Water page) — first certification with a pro to 12 m. Open Water Diver — dive independently with a buddy to 18 m worldwide (typically 3 days on Koh Chang). Want both steps? Our Open & Advanced package stacks certifications in one trip.

Why learn to dive on Koh Chang?

Training happens on real local dive sites — colorful reefs, gentle profiles, and famous spots like the HTMS Chang for later fun dives. Read our Marine Life Guide for what you might see. After certification, many divers add specialty courses or continue toward pro training.
