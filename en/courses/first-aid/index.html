First Aid Koh Chang – 1-day certification - from 4,990 THB

Master essential first aid skills with our comprehensive emergency response course. Learn CPR, rescue breathing, and emergency procedures.

Our First Aid Course is open to both divers and non-divers. It includes full CPR training, emergency response theory, AED usage, and oxygen provision. This certification is required before enrolling in the Rescue Diver course and is ideal for anyone wanting to improve their emergency skills – even outside of diving.

Who can join?

  • Divers and non-divers welcome
  • Required before the Rescue Diver course
  • Ideal for anyone wanting stronger emergency skills

What's Included

  • 1-day course
  • Full theoretical training on-site
  • Practical CPR, AED & emergency scenarios
  • Certification as EFR or SDI First Aid provider
  • Open to divers and non-divers alike
  • Snacks, soft drinks & coffee at the dive center
  • All materials and equipment provided
