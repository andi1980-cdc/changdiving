Learn to Dive in Koh Chang – From Try Dive to Pro

Have you ever dreamed of exploring the underwater world? Moving effortlessly through the water, weightless like walking on the moon, while observing fascinating marine life and its natural behavior? Then you're in the right place.

Learn to dive with Chang Diving Center in the beautiful Mu Koh Chang Marine National Park, Thailand. Our certified SDI, TDI and PADI instructors offer scuba diving courses in Koh Chang for every level – from your very first Try Dive to professional training. We provide internationally recognized certifications, including Open Water Diver, Advanced, Rescue, Divemaster, Instructor and crossover programs.

All training is conducted in small groups, ensuring safety, comfort and real progress at your own pace. During your course, you'll explore some of the most beautiful dive sites around Koh Chang – colorful coral reefs, exciting pinnacles and the legendary HTMS Chang Wreck, Thailand's largest wreck dive. Curious about what you might encounter underwater? Visit our Marine Life Guide.

Not ready to commit to a full course yet? Join one of our Day Trips – from relaxed Fun Dives for certified divers to snorkeling trips for families and beginners. Many divers start with a Try Dive before deciding on certification.

For detailed information about schedules, requirements and preparation, explore our How-to Guides. Questions about travel, equipment or diving in Thailand? Our FAQs have you covered.
