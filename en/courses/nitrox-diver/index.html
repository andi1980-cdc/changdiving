Why Take a Nitrox Course in Koh Chang?

The Nitrox Diver Course in Koh Chang is one of the most valuable specialty certifications for recreational divers in Thailand. We teach this enriched air (EANx) certification here on Koh Chang (Gulf of Thailand)—not on Koh Tao—so your training dives are in Mu Koh Chang National Marine Park. Using enriched air nitrox (EAN32 or EAN36 with up to 40% oxygen), you'll significantly reduce nitrogen buildup, extend your bottom time, and enjoy shorter surface intervals between dives. This makes Nitrox diving perfect for multi-day diving trips in the Mu Koh Chang National Marine Park, especially when exploring deeper dive sites like the HTMS Chang wreck at 30 meters or doing 2-3 dives per day on Koh Chang's vibrant reefs.

See every specialty diving course in Koh Chang we offer — this page is Nitrox; the hub lists wreck, deep, navigation and more.

Our Nitrox certification course in Thailand is taught by experienced SDI, TDI and PADI certified instructors and takes just 1-2 days to complete. The course includes comprehensive theory covering oxygen toxicity, maximum operating depth (MOD) calculations, CNS tracking, and proper gas analysis procedures. You'll learn how to analyze Nitrox tanks, set your dive computer for enriched air, and plan safer repetitive dives. Two optional training dives allow you to practice using Nitrox in real diving conditions in the Gulf of Thailand. Whether you're planning extended diving holidays, underwater photography sessions requiring longer bottom times, or preparing for technical diving courses like Advanced Nitrox and Decompression Procedures, this enriched air specialty course is your essential first step.

Thinking About Technical Diving?

If you’re planning to go deeper into the world of technical diving, the Nitrox Diver Course is your first essential step. It’s a prerequisite for the TDI Advanced Nitrox Course, which allows you to use up to 100% oxygen and opens the door to decompression and extended range diving.

Complete your Nitrox certification now and you’re halfway to becoming a tech diver!

If you also want to understand how these nitrox mixes are produced for technical and recreational diving, consider our Nitrox Gas Blender Course to learn professional gas blending skills.
