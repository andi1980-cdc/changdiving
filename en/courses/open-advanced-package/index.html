Open Water + Advanced Package Koh Chang

The Open Water + Advanced Package in Koh Chang is the complete dive certification solution, taking you from absolute beginner to advanced diver (0 to 30 meters depth) in just 4 days. This best-value combo combines both the Open Water Diver and Advanced Open Water Diver courses into one seamless training package with 2 international certifications and 9 total open water dives in the Mu Koh Chang National Marine Park.

This OWD + AOWD package in Koh Chang is taught by experienced SDI, TDI, and PADI certified instructors using a structured 4-day program: Day 1 covers online theory and 1 pool session (confined water training), then 3 training days with 3 dives each for a total of 9 open water dives (4 Open Water certification dives + 5 Advanced adventure dives) at prime sites including Hin Raab South, Blueberry Hill, and the famous HTMS Chang wreck, with mandatory Deep Dive (30m), Navigation, and popular electives like Peak Performance Buoyancy, Wreck Diving, and Nitrox training.

Our complete dive certification package in Koh Chang, Thailand follows small group training (maximum 5 students per instructor) with personalized attention and professional safety standards throughout. The package combines the comprehensive foundational skills of Open Water (buoyancy control, equipment assembly, emergency procedures, dive planning) with advanced specialty training (deep diving techniques, precision navigation, wreck penetration, enriched air diving), giving you a complete skill set and real-world experience at Thailand's most beautiful dive sites.

This Open Water + Advanced combo package is perfect if you:

  • have never dived before and want to fast-track to advanced certification
  • want the best value – save money compared to booking courses separately
  • have limited time and want maximum results (4 days, 2 certifications)
  • prefer learning progressively from basic to advanced in one continuous program
  • want access to deeper sites (30m) including famous wrecks like HTMS Chang
  • are ready to start fun diving independently after certification

All package courses are conducted from our dive boat with professional equipment, multilingual instructors, and a focus on building real diving confidence. After completing both certifications, you'll be qualified to dive to 30 meters worldwide, explore Koh Chang's 13+ dive sites including deeper wrecks and reefs, and continue with specialty courses or Rescue Diver training. This is the most efficient and cost-effective way to become a fully capable, confident diver.

If you're ready to go from zero to advanced in one complete, professionally structured package in Koh Chang, Thailand, our Open Water + Advanced combo gives you the fastest path to dive independence and the skills to explore the underwater world safely.

FAQ – Your Questions Answered

Do I need any diving experience?

No – this combo package starts with the Open Water course, which is perfect for beginners. You’ll be certified up to 30 meters after completing both levels.

Can I take a break between OWD and AOWD?

This package is designed to be completed in 4 consecutive days. If you prefer more time between courses, please book them separately.

How many dives are included?

You’ll complete 9 dives in total: 4 for the Open Water Diver, and 5 for the Advanced Open Water Diver course.

What if I already have my Open Water certification?

Then you don’t need this package – check out our Advanced Open Water Diver Course instead.

Can I add Nitrox later?

Yes, you can add the Nitrox Specialty (theory only) to your package when booking together with a certification course — from 4,990 THB (SDI) or 5,990 THB (PADI). No extra dives needed: you’ll dive enriched air straight away during your OWD & AOWD course dives.
