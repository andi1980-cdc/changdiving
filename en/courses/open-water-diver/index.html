Open Water Diver Course in Koh Chang

The Open Water Diver certification is your first full scuba qualification – and our Open Water course in Koh Chang gets you certified in just 3 days. This scuba diving course in Thailand gives you the skills and confidence to dive safely anywhere in the world. In just 3 days, you'll complete comprehensive theory, 1 pool session (confined water training), and 4 open water dives at calm, shallow dive sites in the Mu Koh Chang National Marine Park.

Our Open Water diving course in Koh Chang is designed for first-time divers and taught by experienced SDI, TDI and PADI certified instructors. You'll learn essential scuba skills including buoyancy control, equipment assembly, underwater navigation, emergency procedures, and proper dive planning. All training dives are conducted at protected beginner-friendly sites like Hin Raab South and Blueberry Hill, where visibility is excellent and conditions are calm.

This scuba diving course in Koh Chang follows a professional, structured approach with small groups (maximum 5 students per instructor) to ensure personalized attention and safety. You'll start with online theory and pool training on Day 1, then progress to shallow reef dives (maximum 18 meters) on Days 2-3. Each open water dive focuses on specific skills while allowing you to explore Thailand's vibrant underwater world.

This Open Water Diver course in Koh Chang is perfect if:

  • You've never dived before and want professional training
  • You prefer small groups and personal instruction
  • You want to learn in calm, warm tropical waters with excellent visibility
  • You're looking for a recognized international certification (SDI, TDI or PADI)
  • You want to continue with fun dives or advanced courses afterwards

All Open Water courses are conducted from our own dive boat with professional equipment, experienced local instructors, and a focus on safety and environmental awareness. After certification, you'll be qualified to dive to 18 meters worldwide and ready to explore Koh Chang's 13+ dive sites or continue your diving education.

If you're looking for a safe, professional and comprehensive scuba diving course in Koh Chang, Thailand, our Open Water Diver certification gives you the best possible start to your diving journey.
