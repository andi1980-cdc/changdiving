Scuba Diver Course in Koh Chang

The Scuba Diver certification is your first step into the underwater world – and our Scuba Diver course in Koh Chang gets you certified in just 2 days. This is the ideal beginner scuba course in Thailand if you have limited time or want to test the waters before committing to the full Open Water course. In 2 days, you'll complete theory, 1 pool session (confined water training), and 2 open water dives at calm, shallow dive sites in the Mu Koh Chang National Marine Park.

Our Scuba Diver course in Koh Chang is designed for first-time divers and taught by experienced SDI, TDI and PADI certified instructors. You'll learn essential scuba skills including buoyancy control, equipment assembly, underwater navigation, and emergency procedures. All training dives are conducted at protected beginner-friendly sites like Hin Raab South and Blueberry Hill, where visibility is excellent and conditions are calm.

This Scuba Diver course in Koh Chang follows a professional, structured approach with small groups (maximum 4 students per instructor) to ensure personalized attention and safety. You'll start with online theory and pool training on Day 1, then complete 2 open water dives (maximum 12 meters) on Day 2. After certification, you can dive to 12m when supervised by a professional.

This Scuba Diver course in Koh Chang is perfect if:

  • You've never dived before and want a quick first certification
  • You have only 2 days available but still want a real cert card
  • You want to learn in calm, warm tropical waters with excellent visibility
  • You're looking for a recognized international certification (SDI or PADI) you can upgrade later
  • You want to upgrade to the full Open Water Diver or continue with fun dives afterwards

All Scuba Diver courses are conducted from our own dive boat with professional equipment, experienced local instructors, and a focus on safety and environmental awareness. After certification, you'll be qualified to dive to 12 meters when supervised and ready to upgrade to the full Open Water Diver at any time.

If you're looking for a quick, professional first scuba diving certification in Koh Chang, Thailand, the Scuba Diver course is the perfect starting point.

Your Dive Path – From Scuba Diver to Advanced
