Expand Your Dive Skills – Specialty Courses at Chang Diving

At Chang Diving Center, our certified instructors offer exciting specialty scuba diving courses to enhance your skills and unlock new adventures. From Nitrox and Deep Diving to Wreck, Navigation, and Night Diving – each course gives you focused training in real dive environments around Koh Chang.

Tailored Learning – Practical, Personal, Flexible
Most specialties can be completed in just 1–2 days with a lighter theory load compared to full certification courses like Open Water. And here’s the good news: you don’t always need an Advanced certification. Many specialties can be taken right after your Open Water Diver – making them the perfect way to continue your dive journey step by step. For details on prerequisites and course flow, check our Specialty Course Guide.

Why Specialties in Koh Chang?
Our dive sites are the perfect classroom: colorful reefs, dramatic pinnacles, and the famous HTMS Chang wreck. Whether you want to master buoyancy, navigate like a pro, extend your dive time with Nitrox, or experience the thrill of night diving – Koh Chang offers it all.
