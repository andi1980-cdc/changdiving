TDI Sidemount Diver Course Koh Chang – Technical Dual-Cylinder Diving

The TDI Sidemount Diver Course is designed to teach certified divers how to safely utilise side-mounted primary cylinders as an alternative to the traditional back-mounted configuration. Over 2 days and 4 open water dives, you develop the gas management, trim, and problem-solving skills demanded by real technical diving — from the HTMS Chang wreck to complex overhead environments. The course can also be combined with Advanced Nitrox, Decompression Procedures, Extended Range, and Trimix.

TDI Sidemount differs from recreational sidemount courses in its technical emphasis: you work with two independent regulator systems, practice S-Drills specific to sidemount, and master gas management across dissimilar cylinder volumes — the same skills required in wreck penetration and cave environments. This is a TDI certification recognised worldwide for technical diving progression.

Why train TDI Sidemount on Koh Chang?

Koh Chang offers calm Gulf conditions ideal for mastering the exacting buoyancy and trim required in sidemount technical diving — plus direct access to the HTMS Chang wreck for real-world overhead training. Our TDI instructors work in groups of maximum 4 students, giving you focused coaching on every dive.

If you already hold a recreational sidemount certification (SDI/PADI) and want to step into true technical configuration, the TDI Sidemount course is the logical next step on your tech diving pathway.

What You’ll Learn

  • ⚙️ Gas matching procedures — including dissimilar cylinder volumes
  • Gas management and switching with two fully independent cylinder systems
  • S-Drills (out-of-gas and bubble check) specific to sidemount
  • Advanced propulsion: frog kick, modified flutter, backwards kick, helicopter turns
  • Buoyancy control — hover at a fixed point without moving hands or feet
  • Trim — maintain correct body position throughout descent, bottom, and ascent
  • Unclip and attach sidemount cylinders while maintaining trim and buoyancy
  • Regulator switches with and without a mask
  • Deploy lift bag; problem solving: gas hemorrhage, entanglement, loss of visibility
  • Conservation, awareness, and back-referencing techniques

Requirements

  • Minimum Age: 18 years
  • Minimum Certification: Open Water Scuba Diver or equivalent
  • Duration: Minimum 2 days (suggested 4 hrs classroom/briefing)
  • Dives: 3 open water dives minimum (90 min accumulated bottom time) — we include a 4th dive
  • Max group size: 4 students per instructor
  • For the Sidemount + Advanced Nitrox package: A valid recreational Nitrox certification (e.g. SDI/PADI Nitrox) is required as a prerequisite for the Advanced Nitrox course
  • Required equipment: Dual cylinders, 2 independent regulators each with SPG, sidemount BCD, exposure suit, mask, fins, dive computer + backup
