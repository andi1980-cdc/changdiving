Technical Diving Courses in Koh Chang – TDI

Take your diving to the next level. Learn decompression, sidemount, and advanced gas planning with expert instructors in real-world conditions.

What technical divers get at Chang Diving Center

At Chang Diving Center, we offer specialized TDI technical diving courses for divers ready to take scuba to the next level. From extended bottom times to deep and decompression dives, our certified instructors guide you through advanced techniques in a professional, safe, and supportive environment.

Serious Skills – Proven Systems
Learn to plan and execute dives beyond recreational limits using twin sets, stage tanks, and sidemount configurations. Courses include detailed theory, skills training, and open-water sessions tailored to your pace and goals – whether you’re aiming for Deco Procedures, Advanced Nitrox, or Advanced Wreck.

Train Where Conditions Are Real
Koh Chang’s HTMS Chang wreck, sloping reefs, and variable depths provide ideal training grounds for real-world tech dive conditions. You’ll master gas management, redundancy protocols, and emergency procedures – while exploring a dynamic underwater environment.

Prerequisites – Build Your Foundation First
Technical diving requires strong recreational foundations. Before joining, you’ll need specialties such as:
• Deep Diver → required for Deco Procedures
• Nitrox → required for Advanced Nitrox
• Wreck Diver → required for Advanced Wreck

👉 Want to fast-track your training? Take our Deep + Wreck + Nitrox Specialty Package – the perfect foundation bundle for progressing into technical diving.

Our team will help you combine recreational and technical training if you’re missing a prerequisite.

Available Technical Courses at Chang Diving: Intro to Tech, Advanced Nitrox, Decompression Procedures, Advanced Wreck, and our Complete Tech Diving Package.

Planning Matters
Unlike recreational dives, technical dives and courses must be carefully planned. During high season, extended decompression dives may not always be possible due to logistics, weather, or boat schedules. Our instructors will work with you to adjust dive profiles and ensure training is both safe and realistic. Flexibility and patience are key in technical training.

Resources for Technical Divers – Planning, Gas & Conditions

📋 TDI Prerequisites Bundle

🧪 Nitrox & Deco Gas Blending

Safe oxygen handling and blending procedures—understand how your technical mixes are produced and take ownership of cylinder labelling and analysis workflows.

📐 Gas Planning & Consumption

Surface consumption, reserve rules, and real-world cylinder matching—the arithmetic behind dependable run times and turnaround decisions on tech dives.

🚢 Wreck Operations on Koh Chang

Site intelligence for the HTMS Chang and regional wrecks—current, logistics, and what to expect before advanced overhead or decompression wreck training.

🪢 Reels & Guidelines

Line laying, jumps, and recovery tactics for limited visibility and penetration dives—core skills for disciplined wreck and staged-deco workflows.

🤿 Solo Diver Add-On

Formal self-reliance training with redundant gas and emergency drills—an optional but valuable layer when you want tighter margins alongside normal team diving.

🛡️ Training-Time Insurance

Specialist cover for deeper, longer profiles—review DAN options before your technical course block so logistics and medical support match your training plan.

🌤️ Marine Weather

Wind, waves, and sea state for Koh Chang—use live conditions to align boat logistics, surface intervals, and decompression commitments with realistic forecasts.
