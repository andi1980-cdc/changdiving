Phutthayotfa Chulalok Frigate – A New Wreck Diving Adventure near Koh Chang

The Phutthayotfa Chulalok frigate, a former warship of the Royal Thai Navy, will soon be scuttled at a depth of 40 meters near Koh Chang. This impressive wreck offers experienced divers a unique adventure and the chance to explore a piece of maritime history.

Phutthayotfa Chulalok Frigate Details

  • Length: 134 meters overall, 126 meters at the waterline.
  • Beam: 14.25 meters.
  • Draft: 7.54 meters.
  • Displacement: 4,065 tons (standard), 4,260 tons (fully loaded).
  • Propulsion: Combustion Engineering steam propulsion with 35,000 HP (26,000 kW), achieving a speed of 27 knots (50 km/h).
  • Armament (historical):
    • 1 x 127 mm Mark 42 gun.
    • 8-round ASROC launcher for anti-submarine rockets.
    • 4 Mark 32 torpedo tubes (324 mm).
  • Features: Helicopter deck and hangar, later modified for SH-2D Seasprite helicopters.

Dive Site Details

  • Depth: 40 meters – perfect for Deep Diver or technical divers.
  • Location: Approximately 2–5 km south of the HTMS Chang.
  • Access: Boat only.
  • Visibility: Expected to range between 15–30 meters, depending on the season.
  • Marine Life: Tropical fish schools, coral formation, and possible encounters with larger marine species.

What Makes This Dive Site Special?

The Phutthayotfa Chulalok frigate is not just an impressive wreck but also a future artificial reef that will quickly develop into a hotspot for marine life. Its size and structure provide exciting challenges for experienced divers, while its history offers a fascinating insight into maritime technology and warfare.

Requirements

  • Certification: Minimum AOWD (Advanced Open Water Diver) with a Deep Diver specialty or technical diver certification.
  • Experience: At least 30 logged dives, including several at depths over 30 meters.
  • Equipment: Divers with Nitrox certification are highly recommended.

Safety and Preparation

Due to the depth of this wreck, thorough preparation is essential. Ensure you have all necessary certifications and equipment. Nitrox and additional safety gear are available to enhance your experience and safety.
