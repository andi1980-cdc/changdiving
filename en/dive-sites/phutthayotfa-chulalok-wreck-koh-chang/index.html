Phutthayotfa Chulalok Frigate – The Cancelled Wreck Project near Koh Chang

Why this Koh Chang wreck project was cancelled

The Phutthayotfa Chulalok frigate, a former warship of the Royal Thai Navy, was planned to be scuttled at 40 meters depth near Koh Chang to create an artificial reef dive site. However, this project has been cancelled. The required permits were never obtained, and the ship is now being used elsewhere. It will not become a dive site near Koh Chang. We have kept this page as an informational record for divers who may have read about the planned project.

Phutthayotfa Chulalok Frigate Details

  • Length: 134 meters overall, 126 meters at the waterline.
  • Beam: 14.25 meters.
  • Draft: 7.54 meters.
  • Displacement: 4,065 tons (standard), 4,260 tons (fully loaded).
  • Propulsion: Combustion Engineering steam propulsion with 35,000 HP (26,000 kW), achieving a speed of 27 knots (50 km/h).
  • Armament (historical):
    • 1 x 127 mm Mark 42 gun.
    • 8-round ASROC launcher for anti-submarine rockets.
    • 4 Mark 32 torpedo tubes (324 mm).
  • Features: Helicopter deck and hangar, later modified for SH-2D Seasprite helicopters.

Project Status: Cancelled

The scuttling of the Phutthayotfa Chulalok near Koh Chang will not take place. The necessary approvals and permits were not secured, and the ship has since been repurposed elsewhere. There is no planned alternative date or location for this project near Koh Chang.

If you are looking for wreck diving near Koh Chang, please visit our existing wreck dive sites listed below.

What Was Planned

  • Planned Depth: 40 meters – targeted at Deep Diver or technical divers.
  • Planned Location: Approximately 2–5 km south of the HTMS Chang.
  • Access: Would have been boat only.
  • Status: Project cancelled – permits not obtained, ship used elsewhere.
