Dive Courses & Training – Frequently Asked Questions

What dive courses do you offer in Koh Chang?

We offer everything from Try Dives to Instructor – following SDI, TDI, and PADI standards.

Learn more about our course options

Try Dive Info Course Requirements

How long does the Open Water Course take?

Usually 3 to 4 days, depending on your pace and weather conditions. We are flexible with scheduling to ensure you get the most out of your training!

Detailed course timeline

Course Requirements Equipment & Schedule

How does the theory part of my course work?

The theory is 100% online – no classroom needed. You can learn from home or your beach bungalow. It is available in all major languages supported by your chosen training agency (SDI, PADI, TDI, etc.).

Course Registration Study Materials

When will I receive my theory materials?

Usually within 24 hours after we receive your payment. Please check your spam folder too!

Payment Process Course Materials

How do I register for the theory materials?

We'll send you a personalized link to register with SDI, TDI or PADI – depending on your course.

Compare certification agencies

Can I start scuba diving with no experience?

Absolutely! You can join a Try Dive or Open Water Course without any prior experience. Our experienced instructors will guide you every step of the way.

In which languages are the courses available?

The online theory is available in many languages 🌍. Practical diving lessons are conducted in English 🗣️, as we often have international guests from all over the world diving together.

What if I can't finish my course due to health reasons?

No worries – you only pay for the part you've completed. Please let us know early so we can make appropriate arrangements.
