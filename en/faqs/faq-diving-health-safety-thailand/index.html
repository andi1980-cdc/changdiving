Health & Safety – Frequently Asked Questions

Learn everything about medical clearance, dive insurance, underwater vision and our emergency response plans in Koh Chang.

🏥 Do I need a medical certificate to dive?

In most cases, you just need to fill out a medical questionnaire. A doctor's certificate is only needed for specific conditions.

📄 ➜ Open official Medical Questionnaire (PDF)

📋 Diver Medical Self-Check – answer Yes or No

Complete this screening honestly (same questions as the official UHMS / RSTC Medical Questionnaire). Click Yes or No. A Yes opens the related follow-up box. Informational only — not medical advice. If the screening is clear, emailing your confirmed results is enough. If a physician’s evaluation is required, print the Medical Questionnaire PDF and bring the signed dive medical clearance to Chang Diving. If pregnant or trying to become pregnant, do not dive.

Date of birth

  1. I have had problems with my lungs, breathing, heart and/or blood affecting my normal physical or mental performance.

    Box A – I have / have had:

    A Yes here usually means a physician’s evaluation is required.

    1. Chest surgery, heart surgery, heart valve surgery, an implantable medical device (e.g. stent, pacemaker, neurostimulator), pneumothorax, and/or chronic lung disease.

    2. Asthma, wheezing, severe allergies, hay fever or congested airways within the last 12 months that limits my physical activity/exercise.

    3. A problem or illness involving my heart such as: angina, chest pain on exertion, heart failure, immersion pulmonary edema, heart attack or stroke, OR I am taking medication for any heart condition.

    4. Recurrent bronchitis and currently coughing within the past 12 months, OR I have been diagnosed with emphysema.

    5. Symptoms affecting my lungs, breathing, heart and/or blood in the last 30 days that impair my physical or mental performance.

  2. I am over 45 years of age.

    Box B – I am over 45 years of age AND:

    A Yes here usually means a physician’s evaluation is required.

    1. I currently smoke or inhale nicotine by other means.

    2. I have a high cholesterol level.

    3. I have high blood pressure.

    4. I have had a close blood relative die suddenly or of cardiac disease or stroke before the age of 50, OR have a family history of heart disease before age 50.

  3. I struggle to perform moderate exercise (for example, walk 1.6 km / 1 mile in 14 minutes or swim 200 metres without resting), OR I have been unable to participate in normal physical activity due to fitness or health reasons within the past 12 months.

    A Yes here usually means a physician’s evaluation is required.

  4. I have had problems with my eyes, ears, or nasal passages/sinuses.

    Box C – I have / have had:

    A Yes here usually means a physician’s evaluation is required.

    1. Sinus surgery within the last 6 months.

    2. Ear disease or ear surgery, hearing loss, or problems with balance.

    3. Recurrent sinusitis within the past 12 months.

    4. Eye surgery within the past 3 months.

  5. I have had surgery within the last 12 months, OR I have ongoing problems related to past surgery.

    A Yes here usually means a physician’s evaluation is required.

  6. I have lost consciousness, had migraine headaches, seizures, stroke, significant head injury, or suffer from persistent neurologic injury or disease.

    Box D – I have / have had:

    A Yes here usually means a physician’s evaluation is required.

    1. Head injury with loss of consciousness within the past 5 years.

    2. Persistent neurologic injury or disease.

    3. Recurring migraine headaches within the past 12 months, or take medications to prevent them.

    4. Blackouts or fainting (full/partial loss of consciousness) within the last 5 years.

    5. Epilepsy, seizures, or convulsions, OR take medications to prevent them.

  7. I am currently undergoing treatment (or have required treatment within the last five years) for psychological problems, personality disorder, panic attacks, or an addiction to drugs or alcohol; or I have been diagnosed with a learning or developmental disability.

    Box E – I have / have had:

    A Yes here usually means a physician’s evaluation is required.

    1. Behavioral health, mental or psychological problems requiring medical/psychiatric treatment.

    2. Major depression, suicidal ideation, panic attacks, uncontrolled bipolar disorder requiring medication/psychiatric treatment.

    3. Been diagnosed with a mental health condition or a learning/developmental disorder that requires ongoing care or special accommodation.

    4. An addiction to drugs or alcohol requiring treatment within the last 5 years.

  8. I have had back problems, hernia, ulcers, or diabetes.

    Box F – I have / have had:

    A Yes here usually means a physician’s evaluation is required.

    1. Recurrent back problems in the last 6 months that limit my everyday activity.

    2. Back or spinal surgery within the last 12 months.

    3. Diabetes, either drug or diet controlled, OR gestational diabetes within the last 12 months.

    4. An uncorrected hernia that limits my physical abilities.

    5. Active or untreated ulcers, problem wounds, or ulcer surgery within the last 6 months.

  9. I have had stomach or intestine problems, including recent diarrhea.

    Box G – I have had:

    A Yes here usually means a physician’s evaluation is required.

    1. Ostomy surgery and I do not have medical clearance to swim or engage in physical activity.

    2. Dehydration requiring medical intervention within the last 7 days.

    3. Active or untreated stomach or intestinal ulcers or ulcer surgery within the last 6 months.

    4. Frequent heartburn, regurgitation, or gastroesophageal reflux disease (GERD).

    5. Active or uncontrolled ulcerative colitis or Crohn’s disease.

    6. Bariatric surgery within the last 12 months.

  10. I am taking prescription medications (with the exception of birth control or anti-malarial drugs other than mefloquine / Lariam).

    A Yes here usually means a physician’s evaluation is required.

Enter your name and date of birth, finish the self-check, tick the confirmation, then email your answers. If the screening is clear, that email is your declaration. If a physician’s evaluation is required, also print the PDF and see a doctor.

👓 Can I dive with glasses or contact lenses?

Yes! Contact lenses are usually fine. We also offer masks with prescription lenses.

🛡️ Is diving insurance required?

Diving insurance is highly recommended. You can get day or annual coverage at low cost.

🚨 What is the emergency protocol at Chang Diving?

In the rare event of a diving accident, we follow a clear and structured emergency plan. Our staff is trained, we carry first aid and oxygen onboard, and we have direct access to the decompression chamber in Trat.
