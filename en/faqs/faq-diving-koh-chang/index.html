General Questions About Diving in Koh Chang

Everything you should know about diving season, conditions, marine life and dive sites around Koh Chang, Thailand.

What does diving and dive courses cost on Koh Chang?

Prices depend on the activity: guided Fun Dives start from 3,290 THB, a Try Dive from 4,890 THB, and the Open Water Diver course from 14,490 THB. Full current rates for day trips, courses, specialties and tech are on our prices page.

Related: Booking & payment · Dive courses FAQ

What are the prerequisites to learn diving?

For a Try Dive you need no prior diving experience. To start an Open Water course you should be comfortable in the water, able to swim, and fit enough for light physical activity. Minimum age is usually 10 years for junior programmes (with parental consent). We train in small groups with SDI, TDI and PADI.

Related: Course FAQ · Beginner courses

Do I need a medical dive certificate?

In most cases you only complete a medical questionnaire. A doctor’s certificate is needed only if you answer “yes” to certain health questions or have specific conditions. Details and the form are in our health & safety FAQ.

Where does the dive boat depart from?

In high season the boat departs from Klong Prao Beach, just steps from the dive center. We can pick you up from your hotel, or you meet us at the center around 8:00 AM / at the beach departure point around 8:15 AM. A small transfer boat takes you out to our dive boat offshore.

Why is Koh Chang a great diving destination in Thailand?

Warm water, healthy reefs and a relaxed atmosphere make Koh Chang ideal for divers of all levels – from first Try Dive to wreck and technical training.

When is the best time to go diving in Koh Chang?

The main season runs from October to May with calmer seas and typically better visibility. Diving is possible year-round; rainy months can mean fewer boats and more macro opportunities.

What are the water temperatures and visibility like?

Expect water temperatures around 28–30°C, with visibility commonly from about 10 to 20 meters (conditions vary with season and site).

Are there any wrecks or special dive sites in Koh Chang?

Yes – the HTMS Chang wreck is Thailand’s largest wreck dive, plus reefs and pinnacles such as Hin Luk Bat. Browse our dive sites and site map.

What marine life can I see while diving in Koh Chang?

You might spot turtles, moray eels, pufferfish, seahorses, nudibranchs, stingrays and occasionally whale sharks. Koh Chang’s marine biodiversity is excellent for both beginners and experienced divers.
