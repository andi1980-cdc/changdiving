Koh Chang Diving with Chang Diving Center – Trusted Since 2005

Welcome to Chang Diving Center, a 5* Instructor Training Facility (ITF) at Klong Prao Beach since 2005 – your gateway to the best scuba diving in Koh Chang and the Mu Koh Chang Marine National Park. Explore vibrant coral reefs and historic wrecks, schools of tropical fish, sea turtles and – if you’re lucky – a whale shark while diving with us.

Whether you’re a beginner taking your first breaths underwater with a Try Dive, a certified diver looking for fun dives or Nitrox (enriched air) dives in Koh Chang, or an adventurer ready for wreck diving on the HTMS Chang with our specialised recreational and technical dive team – we’ve got you covered.

Our experienced SDI, TDI and PADI instructors offer a full range of scuba diving courses – from Open Water Diver certification up to professional training. Learn in small groups, explore vibrant coral reefs, and build skills with safe, personalized guidance.

With scuba certification in Thailand, you open the door to unforgettable dive sites worldwide. Join us for wreck, reef and advanced courses – or simply enjoy Koh Chang’s colorful marine life on guided day trips.

Plan and book your diving in Koh Chang online today – fast, easy and secure. Discover why divers from all over the world trust Chang Diving for their underwater adventures.
