Your Perfect Ocean Escape – Diving & Blog Koh Chang

Welcome to the Chang Diving Blog – your gateway to the underwater world of Koh Chang’s dive sites. Whether you’re dreaming of your first Try Dive, looking for exciting Fun Dives, or ready to join a scuba diving course, this space is built to inspire, guide, and educate divers of all levels.

Our expert SDI, TDI and PADI instructors run courses and fun dives across wrecks and reefs around Koh Chang. From Open Water certification to Advanced training and even professional programs, you’ll find the guidance you need to progress safely and confidently.

Articles cover a wide range of topics, from how-to dive guides and local marine life to scuba knowledge & theory and practical dive tips. You’ll also discover real talk from the dive deck, where we compare agencies, review dive gear, and share what it’s really like to go pro.

At Chang Diving Center, our mission goes beyond selling dives – we build confident, informed divers who respect the ocean. That’s why our content connects directly to our courses, fun dives and trips, so you know exactly where your next adventure begins.
