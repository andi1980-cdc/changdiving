Koh Chang Diving Travel Guides – Discover Thailand's Hidden Dive Paradise

Adventures for every ocean lover – practical travel and diving guides for Koh Chang, Thailand.

Planning a dive trip to Koh Chang? Our curated travel guides help you explore everything this tropical island has to offer – both above and below the surface. As Thailand's second-largest island and part of the protected Mu Koh Chang National Park, Koh Chang combines stunning jungle scenery, white-sand beaches, and crystal-clear waters with a laid-back, authentic atmosphere.

Whether you're a certified diver, total beginner, or snorkelling enthusiast, our guides cover the best dive sites, travel logistics, marine life, weather conditions, and practical tips to help you make the most of your diving holiday. Learn how to get here, when to dive, what to pack, and what to expect under water – all written by locals and dive pros with years of experience.

Unlike Thailand's overcrowded hotspots, Koh Chang offers relaxed and uncrowded diving – perfect for those seeking personal attention, pristine reefs, and one of the best wreck dives in Southeast Asia: the HTMS Chang Wreck. Our guides also compare Thailand's diving regions (like the Gulf vs. Andaman), reveal insider tips, and recommend top activities, beaches, and accommodations.

Start planning your perfect scuba getaway to Koh Chang with our expert travel guides – your gateway to Thailand's best-kept diving secret.
