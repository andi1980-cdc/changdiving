Marine Life in Koh Chang – Tropical Species & Dive Encounters

From tiny nudibranchs to barracudas and turtles – what you can actually see on Koh Chang reefs.

Koh Chang's marine life is as vibrant and diverse as the island itself. Nestled within the Mu Ko Chang National Park, the coral reefs and underwater ecosystems around the island are home to hundreds of species – from tiny nudibranchs to majestic whale sharks.

Whether you're joining a Fun Dive, completing your Open Water Diver course, or simply snorkeling, the crystal-clear waters of Koh Chang offer unforgettable encounters with tropical marine creatures. Expect to meet colorful reef fish like angelfish, parrotfish, and butterflyfish, alongside fascinating macro life such as seahorses and ghost pipefish. For wreck diving fans, the HTMS Chang is not only Thailand’s largest shipwreck but also a thriving artificial reef where barracudas, giant groupers, and even turtles regularly appear.

Our Marine Life category dives deep into the species you're most likely to encounter while exploring Koh Chang’s dive sites. Learn to recognize different fish families, understand their behaviors, and discover where and when to find them. From elusive octopuses and venomous scorpionfish to eagle rays and schooling fusiliers – each article is packed with insights for nature lovers and underwater photographers alike.

Many of these creatures appear right during your scuba diving courses or day trips. Training dives often double as amazing encounters – spotting clownfish on your first Try Dive, barracudas on your Advanced course, or eagle rays while practicing navigation. That’s what makes learning to dive on Koh Chang so unique: education and adventure go hand in hand.

With warm waters and year-round biodiversity, Koh Chang is the perfect destination to experience Thailand’s marine richness in a relaxed, crowd-free environment. Whether you’re preparing for a dive or just fascinated by ocean life, let this section be your gateway to the wonders beneath the surface.
