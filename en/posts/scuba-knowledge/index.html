Expand Your Scuba Diving Knowledge in Koh Chang

From SAC rates and equipment to safety and dive planning – practical guides from beginner to pro.

Scuba diving is more than just an underwater adventure – it’s a skill, a science, and a lifestyle. At Chang Diving Center, we help divers at every level, from beginners to pros, build real-world knowledge that boosts safety, confidence, and enjoyment under water.

In this section you’ll find clear explanations of core topics like SAC rate & gas consumption, Nitrox basics, buoyancy & trim, dive planning & logbook use, and safety procedures (e.g., SMB). Complex theory is broken down into simple, practical steps – perfect prep for your Open Water course, a relaxed Fun Dive, or future technical training.

Apply what you learn on our Koh Chang dive sites – from colorful reefs to the HTMS Chang wreck. For planning your trip, pair these articles with our How-to Guides, Marine Life, and FAQs.
