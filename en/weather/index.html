Beyond the Forecast – Plan Your Koh Chang Dive Trip

When you check the weather for Koh Chang, you're not just planning a beach day – you're imagining an underwater adventure. Will the sea be calm enough for the boat? How's the visibility? Will you get to dive the HTMS Chang wreck, drift past Koh Rang's reefs, or spot a turtle in the blue? These questions matter to every diver who dreams of Koh Chang's crystal waters. Here's what we've learned from years of diving here: Koh Chang is diveable year-round. What matters most isn't rain – tropical showers are brief and often followed by sunshine – but wind and waves. Our Windguru forecasts above give you the real picture. So you've checked the weather – now what? Below you'll find everything you need to turn that forecast into an unforgettable dive: FAQs about the island and diving conditions, dive sites, marine life, travel guides, and courses. Whether you're a first-time snorkeler or a seasoned diver, we've got you. Plan your dive – and dive your plan.
