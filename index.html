Professional scuba diving center in Koh Chang, Thailand. Our PADI, SDI and TDI certified instructors offer diving courses, fun dives, and day trips for all levels.

At Chang Diving Center our certified SDI, TDI and PADI instructors guide you through adventure-packed snorkeling tours, relaxed fun dives, confidence-building Try Dives and refresher Scuba Review programs around the dive sites of Koh Chang, including the iconic HTMS Chang wreck.

Beginners start with the Open Water Diver course and can progress seamlessly to the Advanced Open Water Diver course, First Aid training and hands-on Rescue Diver course. Specialty enthusiasts enjoy our Specialty Diver courses, while career-focused divers move into Divemaster internships, the professional EFR Instructor course, SDI Instructor Development Courses (IDC) and Instructor Crossover pathways.

To plan your trip, explore the latest price list, browse the full catalogue of scuba diving courses, and review our general Koh Chang FAQs along with answers on diving logistics, Try Dives & Fun Dives, courses and training, health & safety, equipment logistics, booking & payments and travel & accommodation. For stories and insights, visit our diving blog or contact our team for personalized trip planning.

Stay connected with daily photos, events and live updates on our official social channels: follow Chang Diving Center on Facebook and @changdivingcenter on Instagram.
